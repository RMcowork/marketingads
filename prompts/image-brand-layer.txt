A studio-style product photograph for a burger restaurant's social media. The burger
is the hero, in the middle of the frame, on a pale warm-grey stone counter in front of
a softly mottled light-grey marble wall. Clean and minimal, no clutter. Soft, even,
diffused light from the front-left with gentle shadows. The brioche bun is deep
amber-brown and glossy, the patty thick and seared, the cheese melting and running.
The food looks real, a little messy and appetising, never plastic. Colours stay within
warm greys, stone, amber and brown, with at most a small touch of deep maroon or tan.
Shallow depth of field. The camera angle and anything else in the frame come from the
scene description.
